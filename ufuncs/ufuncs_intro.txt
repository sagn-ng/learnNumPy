- NumPy ufuncs (universal functions) are fast, vectorized functions
that perform element-wise operations on NumPy arrays.
- They are highly optimized and support features like broadcasting and automatic type handling.
- NumPy includes many ufuncs for arithmetic, trigonometry, statistics, etc, and they execute
operations very fast because they are implemented in optimized C code.
